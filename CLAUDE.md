## Project Overview

This is a Chinese academic book project titled "《这才是医美消费心理学》" (This is Medical Aesthetics Consumer Psychology). The project consists of 10 core chapters plus 2 research documents, totaling approximately 500,000 words. The book explores the psychological aspects of medical aesthetics consumption from multiple stakeholder perspectives.

## Content Architecture

### Four Stakeholder Perspectives
The book analyzes medical aesthetics consumption from four key perspectives:
1. **Consumer Perspective** - Focus on decision-making, risk assessment, and psychological needs
2. **Doctor Perspective** - Professional judgment, ethical considerations, and service delivery
3. **Sales Personnel Perspective** - Communication strategies, ethical marketing, and relationship building
4. **Investor Perspective** - Industry trends, risk management, and sustainable development

### Research Methodologies
- Quantitative research methods
- Qualitative research methods
- Mixed research methods
- Action research methods

### Key Features
- 30+ professional assessment tools
- 10+ in-depth case studies
- Evidence-based approach with empirical data
- Critical thinking emphasis inspired by "This is Psychology" methodology
